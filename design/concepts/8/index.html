What matters more than speed

  • No trade callsPositive or negative is an interpretation of the news, not a buy or sell signal.
  • Evidence attachedHow many outlets reported it, links to the original article or official notice, and when it was updated.
  • Honest about errorsArticle calls were validated on 1,000 pre-labelled stories; newswire calls are being validated the same way. The AI can be wrong.
