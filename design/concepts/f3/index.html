Questions

If it says positive, will the price go up?

No. It’s how the news is generally read, not a forecast. In our own records, prices after news looked much like any other hour.

How accurate is the AI?

Article judgments were validated against 1,000 pre-labelled stories. Newswire judgments are being validated the same way. It can be wrong, and we say so.
